# Using Garage Design

Garage Design turns garage measurements and a visual tile pattern into a product-specific purchase
plan. Work from the top of the page downward because later sections depend on the choices above
them.

## 1. Measure the garage

Enter the inside width of the front wall and the inside depth of the garage in inches. The default
garage is 230 inches wide by 246 inches deep.

The expansion clearance is removed from every wall before tile fit is calculated. With the default
one-inch clearance, the default garage has a 228 by 244 inch tile field.

## 2. Describe the garage doors

Choose one double door, two single doors, three single doors, or a custom sequence of openings and
wall sections. Door widths determine where the openings appear and how much transition ramp is
needed.

The horizontal top edge of every plan is the garage-door edge.

## 3. Choose and customize a design

Browse the 250 layouts by category or search by name. Categories include borders, checkerboards,
parking bays, stripes, chevrons, center fields, racing patterns, and showroom layouts.

Choose **Custom** to repaint the design:

- Select the base, accent, or secondary paint role.
- Click or tap one square to paint it.
- Drag across several squares with a mouse, touch screen, or pen.
- Use the arrow keys to move the keyboard cursor.
- Press Enter or Space to paint the selected square.
- Use **Clear painted squares** to return to the underlying preset.

The conceptual design grid is mapped to the selected product's actual tile dimensions. A product
is disabled when it cannot supply every color role required by the design.

## 4. Compare tile products

The planner offers only products whose published top surface is open, perforated, or described as
self-draining. Each product entry identifies its dimensions, published colors, package options,
dated source links, drainage evidence, and any product-specific caveats.

The preview shows how the selected design maps to that product. Product color names are preserved,
while the on-screen swatches are approximations because vendors do not publish exact digital color
values.

## 5. Review fit and cuts

Garage Design anchors full tiles to the front and right edges of the clearance-reduced tile field:

- The front edge of the usable tile field receives full, uncut tiles.
- The right edge of the usable tile field receives full, uncut tiles.
- Width remainder becomes a cut strip on the left.
- Depth remainder becomes a cut strip at the back.
- A separate back-left corner piece is listed when both dimensions require cuts.

The estimate does not assume that one offcut can satisfy a second edge unless that reuse is
explicitly modeled.

## 6. Review the purchase plan

The summary reports:

- placed whole and cut tiles
- waste allowance by color
- required quantity by color
- boxes, packs, or individual tiles to purchase
- the lowest-cost verified package combination
- leftover tiles by color
- transition-ramp pieces and packages for each door opening
- merchandise subtotal
- Illinois state tax at 6.25%
- verified shipping or an unknown-shipping warning

Shipping is never assumed to be free. When shipping is unknown, Garage Design shows the known
merchandise and tax total but does not present a final checkout total.

## 7. Save or export

Saved plans remain in browser storage on the current device. A saved plan preserves dimensions,
door configuration, clearance, layout, painted cells, colors, waste allowance, and selected
product.

You can export:

- a high-resolution PNG of the exact floor drawing
- a print-friendly project report that can be printed or saved as PDF

Remote third-party product photos are excluded from exported files.

## Before ordering

Recheck every seller link. Product prices, promotions, stock, colors, shipping policies, and
accessory compatibility can change after the catalog's checked date. The planner excludes tools,
subfloor repair, preparation materials, installation labor, and seller-specific tax treatment.
